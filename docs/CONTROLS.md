# Controls

Every action, with its keyboard key, controller button and touch control. Keyboard and controller work together: per control, the stronger input wins. Browsers only expose a controller after its first button press; the HUD then shows "Xbox controller connected" (or PlayStation / Controller).

Hotkeys leave Ctrl / Cmd / Alt chords to the browser (Ctrl+R reloads, Ctrl+C copies) and skip keys typed into a text field, select or editable area.

## Touch screens

On a coarse pointer (phones, tablets) the controls sit in the two bottom corners and a strip at the top right, at the screen's edges and inside the safe areas (a notch, rounded corners and the home-indicator bar never sit under one). They are drawn as a racing game draws them: round, translucent touch zones with a thin ring and a glyph (a word under it only where the glyph is not obvious), never bordered boxes with labels. Nothing persistent is ever drawn in the road zone, the middle half of the width from 15 % to 70 % of the height. Desktop browsers never show any of it. The controls write the same controller state a gamepad does (`GamepadInput.touch`, merged into `PadState` on every poll), so every controller binding below works by touch too, and keyboard, controller and touch mix freely. Every control is at least 44 px (`npm run check:phone-hud` checks this, the road zone, overlaps, the insets, real multi-touch, fullscreen and the disc's small pushes on a Pixel 7 and an iPhone 14, both ways up).

- **Control schemes** (Pause card, or Menu → Settings → Controls; stored in the browser; **Left-handed** swaps the two corners):
  - **Disc** (the default): a round **driving disc** lower left, "Go" on its upper ring and "Brake" on its lower. One thumb does everything: push up to go, down to brake and then reverse, sideways to steer, and any amount of it: a third of the way is a third of the gas, the brake or the wheel (`discReading` in `game/hud/touch-scheme.ts`; the pad's controller-stick deadzone and curve are undone for it, so a small nudge is not swallowed). Boost and Handbrake are the right thumb's, beside the drive dial.
  - **Pedals**: a **steering slider** lower left (slide the knob; it springs back), a green **Gas** pedal (the corner) and a **Brake** pedal beside it.
  - **Easy**: the slider and a big **Brake** lower right, over it an **Auto gas** light, green while the car pulls by itself. Gas is on whenever Brake is not held. Outside a race the car waits until the first touch on the slider or Brake (so following a car or a Mini game's run-up does not start by itself).
  - **Tilt**: tilt the phone like a wheel (the first reading, and a tap on the bar, set the level); gas as in Easy, **Brake** lower right. Settings has the two knobs: the tilt for full lock (12 to 45 degrees, default 25) and the dead zone (0 to 8 degrees, default 2). iPhone Safari asks for the motion sensor from a tap ("Enable tilt sensor"). `game/hud/tilt-steer.ts` explains each number.
  - Steering and pedals each track their own finger, so steering while braking, boosting or holding the handbrake works. The pedals feed the pad's triggers (`TouchPad.gas` / `.brake`), merged after the stick's deadzone.
- **Buttons** (right thumb, above the drive dial's corner): **Boost** (hold) and **Handbrake** (hold), round with the word under the glyph. The drive dial (speed, gear, revs, boost bottle) is in the bottom-right corner as it always was, in a race and in a derby, in both orientations; the pedals stand above it. While the Long jump's bail can still be pressed, Boost grows to 88 x 72 px: it is the control the prompt names.
- **Fullscreen** (top right corner of every screen, a phone on its side shows it as a pill with its word until fullscreen is on, then a round icon; the Pause card has its own, and so does the Menu sheet): one tap, no menu. Nothing the game does between scenes leaves fullscreen; where the browser can't (iPhone Safari) the button is not drawn.
- **Top strip** (top right beside Fullscreen, 48 px round buttons, only where they act): **View**, **Rear** (hold to look back), **Pause** in a race or **Exit** out of one; **Respawn** (race) / **Recover** (sandbox) appears at the strip's left end only while the car needs it, glowing. **Prev** and **Next** are drawn only while a car is being watched (not the whole field, not at the wheel) and the cycle has somewhere to go: two or more targets (`watchTargets` in the HUD state, counted by the list Q/E and LB/RB walk; `game/hud/watch-cycle.ts`), and take no room otherwise; the race's Spectating bar follows the same rule. **Free** / **Exit** show only where there is somewhere to step out to. In a portrait phone the strip wraps to a second row beside the Menu cluster's room.
- **On the 3D view**: one finger drags to orbit (it looks round the car while driving), two fingers pinch to zoom, and a tap on a car follows it. The Lab and the garage have no seat: a tap, Q/E or a pedal never follows or drives a car there. The garage's orbit never turns by itself, View does nothing in it, it circles the driver's chest while **Driver** is picked (the car's shot while **Car** is), and the wheel and a pinch come in to 0.65 m from his chest and 3 m from the car's shot, where the near plane cuts no body (`minRadius` in `GARAGE`; the fleet's closest is 4.2 m); it holds the player's car alone, the fleet's others are built again on leaving it.
- **Menu cluster** (top left, outside a race): **Menu**, **Reset**, **Play / Pause**; the Online chip beside it when you are not driving. **Menu** is a full-screen sheet (`home-sheet.tsx`, fades in over the live view, **Back** or **Resume** returns): the mode cards (Race, Derby, Survival, Range, Garage, Free play), the test rigs (Press, Pistons, Doors, Corkscrew, Stack, the Lab, Benchmark; muted, they are not game modes), Settings, Credits and **Fullscreen** (the Fullscreen API with the webkit fallback; browsers that can't, such as iPhone Safari, don't show it). The first visit to a bare address opens it once. In a race the Pause card has the same **Modes** button. Android shows its own "to exit full screen" notice for a few seconds after Fullscreen: it is the system's and sits over whatever is at the bottom.
- **Settings** are a docked sheet (`sheet.tsx`): the right half of a phone on its side, the bottom 70 % of an upright one, so a slider's effect can be watched beside it. **Controls** is its first section. The panel is never open on load.
- **Online** is a full-screen sheet with Back; the chip never shows while you drive.
- **Scene panels** (derby board, range card, Mini games, rigs, Lab, garage) live in one 13 rem column at the left under the Menu cluster, scroll inside, and the rigs start folded to a header row. The **Mini game card** is a few lines: the title and the reading (the score once scored), the bail's prompt, and, once scored, **Retry** and **Reset run**; the other modes, the set length and **Exit** are in a docked sheet behind its menu button. From the run-up through the flight to the score the HUD is minimal as in a race (the Menu and Reset buttons, the card, the driving controls; the card and the cluster fade when calm) and returns at the score. The Mini games and the derby open on the far chase view (V cycles it; a view you pick stays). The **Garage**'s panel sits at the bottom edge upright (the car stays in view); picking **Driver** frames and orbits the driver, **Car** frames the set. Every garage colour (the car's parts, the driver's shirt, trousers, hair and cap, the spray can) is one row whose swatch opens the same picker (`ColourRow`, react-colorful): the 15 quick colours, then any colour on a saturation field, a hue bar and a hex box, live while dragged; 44 px targets on a touch screen, above the docked panel on an upright phone and beside the panel otherwise. A spray colour off the quick ones takes one of its bitmap's 15 inks (`SprayBitmap.inkOf`), which is saved and sent with the look. **Symmetric** (under the spray colour, off when the page loads, kept while it stays open) paints each puff a second time mirrored across the body's left-right plane, in the car's or the driver's rest frame (`Garage.restPoint`), so a stroke on one door or sleeve lands on the other too; it is one stroke to undo. **Undo** and **Redo** under it (on a phone the folded panel keeps an undo button; on a keyboard Ctrl/Cmd+Z, and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y) step back and forth through the strokes on what the panel edits, a stroke being a press to its release and **Clear** one step too; each step is saved like a stroke, a new stroke drops the redo chain, a bitmap keeps its last 100 (`SprayUndo`: the changed texels only) and leaving the garage drops them. The **Lab** is seen from the side of the throw in either orientation (a press on a car, dummy or prop flicks it, a press elsewhere orbits with one finger, two fingers pinch) and the view stays put through a flick.
- **Calm**: while you drive (a live race or the wheel in your hands) and nothing has changed for 4 s, the read-outs, the title line and the dial fade to 35-60 %; a pass, a lap, a crash, a banner or a tap on the view brings them back. Touches on the driving controls do not count (`data-keep-idle`), or holding them would keep the HUD lit. Reduced motion makes the fade instant (`game/hud/hud-collapse.ts`, the `calm:` variant in `styles.css`).

A tap shorter than one frame still registers (the press is latched until the next poll).

### Race HUD (focus view)

On a touch screen the read-outs are text straight on the view in the top left (position, lap, race time; a thin line under it with the completion %, last, best, the split and, on an upright phone, the speed and gear), the standings under them show for 3 s after your place changes (and at the grid, the first 4 s after green, at the finish and while spectating), the start lights are one 120 px strip at the top centre and the status banners stack under it; a phone on its side puts the 96 px **drive cluster** at the bottom centre between the thumb corners. Otherwise: top left the standings with gaps, top right lap, position, the race time beside the **race completion %** (share of laps × lap length covered, the measure positions rank by), lap / last / best and the split. On a wide screen without a touch pad the **drive cluster** sits bottom right (a narrow window keeps the compact speed / gear / boost line under the readouts):

- **Rev dial**: speed in the centre (mph with a US locale, else km/h), the gear under it. The revs are faked from the speed inside the gear's bucket: low at the bucket's foot, the redline at its top, falling back at every upshift.
- **Damage arc** (left of the dial): the weaker of the engine block's health and the wheels still on, amber under 60 %, red under 25 %. Under it the **wrench**: lit while a reset would be accepted (R, D-pad ↓), dim in a no-reset race and while spectating, and glowing like the other reset controls once two wheels are gone.
- **Nitrous bottle**: the boost meter (blue fill, glowing while there is a burst in it, white while burning; "Draft" above it while drafting). While the car slides, a green (amber after a contact) segment grows on top of the fill, the share the slide would pay if it ended clean, with "Drift +N%" above the bottle; the payout flashes the bottle green. Hidden where this browser does not hold the meter.
- **Pursuit strip** (bottom centre): "Chased by N cops" while police chase the viewed car (a police race or Survival), with the bust hold and the seconds left while a cop holds the car slow. Hidden otherwise.
- **Near-goal banner** (top centre): "N yd remaining" (mph) or "N m remaining" (km/h) for the last 500 of the last lap.

## Shareable URL

The page's `#` records what you changed: change a scene or setting and the address bar updates (no reload; no history entry for a change inside the scene, **one entry when you enter another scene**, so Back returns to the scene before with the settings it was left with, instead of stepping through slider moves); copy it, and anyone who opens it gets the same scene, settings and initial conditions. Only what differs from the defaults is written. **A page opened with no `#` keeps none**, and one opened with a partial `#` keeps it exactly as it is: loading never writes defaults, the run's seed, or anything a scene sets for itself. The first change writes the changed settings with the run's seed:

```
#cars=5&smin=12&ramps=1&night=1&seed=3fa2c1
```

Put every setting back to its default and the `#` is empty again (`followShare`, `src/game/hud/share-url.ts`).
