> Question: Compare masses: a Monster Jam truck wheel, 66-inch tire and planetary hub assembly (kg) versus a passenger sedan's road wheel and tire (kg); and the Monster Jam truck's gross mass versus a 1500 kg sedan. Give per-corner unsprung mass for both if available.

A Monster Jam corner is roughly **10–25 times heavier** than a passenger-sedan wheel-and-tire assembly, while the complete truck is about **3.6 times heavier** than a \(1500\ \mathrm{kg}\) sedan.

| Comparison | Monster Jam truck | 1500 kg passenger sedan |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tire alone | \( \approx 211\ \mathrm{kg} \) | Typically \(8\text{–}10\ \mathrm{kg}\) for a sedan tire[1] |
| Wheel + tire | \( \approx 293\ \mathrm{kg} \) using the commonly quoted 645 lb mounted mass[2] | Typically \(15.9\text{–}24.9\ \mathrm{kg}\) per corner[3] |
| Wheel + tire + planetary hub assembly | **Approximately \(350\text{–}410\ \mathrm{kg}\)** estimated | Usually included in a broader unsprung assembly, but not normally specified separately |
| Gross/curb mass | \( \approx 5{,}443\ \mathrm{kg}\)[4] | \(1{,}500\ \mathrm{kg}\) |

## Per-corner unsprung mass

- **Monster Jam truck:** approximately **\(350\text{–}410\ \mathrm{kg}\) per corner**, if the planetary hub, wheel, tire, and other fully wheel-carried hardware are included. This is an estimate rather than a published factory breakdown. The tire/wheel alone is independently reported around \(293\ \mathrm{kg}\), while another estimate gives \(360\text{–}408\ \mathrm{kg}\) for a tire-and-wheel assembly.[2][4]
- **Passenger sedan:** approximately **\(40\ \mathrm{kg}\) per corner** is a reasonable representative value for a \(1500\ \mathrm{kg}\) car; one published vehicle model lists \(160\ \mathrm{kg}\) total unsprung mass, or \(40\ \mathrm{kg}\) per corner.[5]
- **Strictly comparable wheel/tire-only figure:** Monster Jam \(\approx293\ \mathrm{kg}\) versus sedan \(\approx16\text{–}25\ \mathrm{kg}\), so the Monster Jam wheel-and-tire assembly is roughly **\(12\text{–}18\times\)** heavier.

“Unsprung mass” normally includes the wheel and tire, brake hardware, hub/knuckle, and portions of suspension components—not merely the tire and rim.[6] The Monster Jam estimate is therefore sensitive to whether the planetary reduction hub and part of the axle housing are counted as unsprung; a published component-level figure for that assembly does not appear to be available.
