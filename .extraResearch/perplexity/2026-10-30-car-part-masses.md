### How to use the figures

- **Bare replacement panel:** use the lower half of the range.
- **OEM part listing:** treat the listed shipping weight as roughly **10–30% above** the actual part unless the listing explicitly says “part weight.”
- **Complete door or bumper:** confirm what is included. A “bumper” may mean only the painted plastic cover, while an “assembly” may include absorber, reinforcement, brackets, grille, lamps, sensors, and wiring.
- **Quarter panel:** a true factory quarter panel is normally welded into the body; aftermarket “quarter skins” are often partial skins and can weigh considerably less than a full replacement quarter section.
- **Wheel assemblies:** published estimates place ordinary passenger-car assemblies around **35–55 lb / 16–25 kg**, and SUV/light-truck assemblies around **60–90+ lb / 27–41+ kg**.[1][2][3]

The cited search material directly supports the wheel-and-tire ranges and the distinction between bumper covers, absorbers, and reinforcement beams; the body-panel values should be treated as representative ranges that require verification against the specific vehicle and OEM catalog listing.
