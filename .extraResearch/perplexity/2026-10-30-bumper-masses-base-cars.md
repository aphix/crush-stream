> Question: Bumper masses in kg for these specific vehicles, front and rear separately, as the whole bumper that would tear off in a crash (face bar or plastic cover plus reinforcement beam/absorber and brackets), with sources (OEM part numbers and shipping weights, restoration/aftermarket listings, salvage data) and saying what each figure includes: (1) 1970 Chevrolet Nova SS front and rear chrome steel bumpers; (2) early-2000s Ford Ranger (1998-2011) front bumper and rear step bumper (chrome or painted steel); (3) 2006 Toyota Corolla (E130 / 9th gen) front and rear bumper (cover plus reinforcement and absorber); (4) Range Rover (L322, 2002-2012, and L405) front and rear bumper assemblies; (5) Volkswagen Golf GTI (Mk5/Mk6) and a Peugeot 206/207/208 GTi hatchback, front and rear bumper assemblies; (6) Ford Crown Victoria Police Interceptor front and rear bumper assemblies, plus a typical steel police push bumper / push bar (Setina, Go Rhino) mass. Concise table: vehicle, front kg, rear kg, includes.

The figures below are **engineering estimates**, not universally published specifications: manufacturers and parts sellers often publish weights for only the cover, beam, or boxed shipment—not the complete removed assembly. “Front” and “rear” therefore mean the bumper components that normally come off with the bumper, excluding fenders, lamps, grille, exhaust, and body brackets left on the vehicle.

### How to use the figures

For a vehicle being weighed after a crash, use the **upper end** when the beam, brackets, sensors, grilles, and substantial hardware remain attached. Use the **lower end** when the cover, absorber, and beam have been separated or when the listing’s “bumper weight” refers only to the face bar.

The **1970 Nova and Ranger estimates are substantially higher** because their traditional chrome/steel bumper bars are structural, whereas the Corolla, Golf, Peugeot, and newer Range Rover assemblies rely heavily on a relatively light plastic cover and foam absorber. A Crown Victoria with a push bar should add approximately **19–25 kg** to the front-end bumper mass; the Go Rhino example supports the middle of that range.[1]
